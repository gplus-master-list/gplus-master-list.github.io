About Basketball Stars

Basketball Stars drops you into quick 1v1 matches on a half court. Pick your character and try to outscore your opponent before time runs out. The game rewards both timing and court vision — you can fake a shot to pull the defender off balance, drive for a layup, or step back for a three if the angle is right.

Matches run fast, usually under two minutes, which makes it easy to fit a few rounds into a short break. The 1v1 format strips out everything except your decisions — no teammates, no excuses, just reads and reactions.

Controls

Player 1 uses arrow keys to move and Z to shoot or steal. Player 2 uses WASD and B. In single-player, arrow keys control your character against an AI opponent.

Tips

Use the shot fake. Hold the shoot button briefly without releasing to bait your opponent into jumping. Once they are in the air, drive past them and finish at the rim.

Position beats reaction on defense. If you are already between the ball and the basket, your opponent runs out of easy angles. Stay central rather than chasing the ball handler to the perimeter.
