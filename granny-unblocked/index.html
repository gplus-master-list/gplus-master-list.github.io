About Granny

Granny Unblocked is a first-person survival horror game where you wake up trapped inside a house with five days to find a way out. The house holds locked doors, hidden items, and puzzles that connect in ways you discover through exploration. Granny herself moves slowly but hears everything — drop anything on the floor and she comes straight to the sound.

Item locations are randomized each run, which gives Granny its replay value. You cannot memorize a fixed route and repeat it. Every attempt involves genuine searching, and searching takes time and makes noise. That tension between needing to move and needing to stay quiet is what the game is built around.

Controls

WASD or arrow keys to move. Mouse to look around. Left click to pick up or interact with objects. Hold Shift to crouch and reduce the noise you make. Touch controls are available on mobile.

Tips

Learn the house layout before trying to escape. Your first few runs should focus on which rooms connect to which, where the beds and wardrobes are, and which objects make the most noise when dropped.

Use hiding spots before Granny has line of sight. If you hear her moving toward your area, get under a bed or into a wardrobe early. Waiting until she is in the same room is almost always too late.
