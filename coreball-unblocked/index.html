About Coreball

Coreball has one of the simplest setups in arcade gaming: a circular core spins in the center of the screen with balls attached to it, and you shoot more balls from below. Your ball cannot touch any of the existing ones. Clip a single ball on the way in and the run ends.

The difficulty builds quickly. The core changes rotation speed between levels, sometimes reversing mid-spin. Later stages have so many balls attached that the gaps are barely wide enough to pass through. Finding the right moment to fire requires patience — rushed shots almost always end runs.

Controls

Click or press space to fire a ball toward the core. There is no aiming — balls always travel straight up. The entire game is about timing that single input.

Tips

Commit to your shot once you see the gap. Hesitating after you start the motion leads to a late shot that clips an existing ball. Once the gap is there and you decide to shoot, do it immediately.

Factor in rotation speed alongside gap size. A wide gap at high rotation speed gives you less time than a narrow gap at slow speed. Always account for how fast the core is moving, not just how large the opening looks.
